THE TECHNOLOGY

Natural movement, made playable.

VisionPuzzle runs hand tracking in your browser with MediaPipe Hands. The camera feed is processed into 21 hand landmarks, then a gesture engine measures finger extension, pinch distance, and movement over time.

Pinch selects and drags. Two fingers rotates. An open palm pauses. A fist asks to reset. Your image never needs to leave the browser for tracking.
